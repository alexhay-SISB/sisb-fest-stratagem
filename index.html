How to play

One device. One tab. One festival per team.

Start your festival

Get set up

  1. You are already in the game. Use the chapter menu to find your next step. Close this guide to make your choices, then reopen it whenever you need help.
  2. Choose who will click, who will check the money and who will check the schedule. Discuss each decision together.
  3. Look below the festival map for the numbered buttons. Start at 1 International. If the browser shows an old game and your teacher wants a fresh start, click Reset game at the top and confirm. It clears the save on that browser.
  4. Keep playing in the same tab. Progress is saved in that browser. Each team opens the same link on its own device.

Know what you need to win

At least 3,001 actual admissions, at least THB 100,000 available for charity, and a festival that finishes safely. Your festival runs from 15:00 to 21:00.

If you need more cash

  1. Find Budget desk in the current step. Click Borrow THB 250,000 (+THB 5,000 fee) once per amount you need. The fee is 2%. You get THB 245,000 more working cash after the fee. You can borrow at most THB 1,000,000 in total.

Game step 1 • International

Book your headliner

Make the first offer

  1. Click 1 International. Scroll down past Budget desk to the act cards.
  2. Read Estimated audience appeal and Guide fee. Appeal is a game score that affects ticket demand. The guide fee is a starting point for negotiation.
  3. For this example, find Tilly Birds. The guide fee is THB 365,000. Click the Offer in baht box and enter 292000. Type digits only, without commas. The game may already have this number filled in.
  4. Click Submit bid on that same card. Read the message above the buttons.

Read the reply and finish the booking

Reply on screenWhat to do
Agent declined. Counteroffer …Read the amount the agent suggests. Replace your old bid with that amount, then click Submit bid again. For example, if the agent asks for THB 378,000, enter 378000.
Offer acceptedClick Confirm booking on the same card. An accepted offer alone is not a booking.
BookedCheck that this message shows your agreed price and that the artist count has gone up to 1.
  1. Scroll to the bottom of the act list. Click Next · Local acts. If it is greyed out, go back to the headliner card and look for Confirm booking.

Before you spend more

You can book one headliner. A different offer might cost much more. Overseas headline acts add THB 55,000 for travel. Changing a confirmed booking with Cancel (20% fee) costs 20% of its fee.
Stray Kids have an in-game appeal score of 50,000 and can fill the festival quickly at suitable prices. Their guide fee is THB 2,400,000. Use Tilly Birds to follow this example and compare the risk of the more expensive act on another run.

Game step 8 • Event day

Deal with four incidents

Make one decision per incident

  1. Read the Incident 1 of 4 card and the numbers above it. Look at cost and tickets at risk on each option. Lost admissions may cause refunds or cost you a sponsor payment.
  2. Agree on a choice, then click Choose response on that card. The game applies the decision and moves to the next incident. Read Last incident to see what happened.
IncidentWorked example button card
1. A wave arrives at Gate 5Open a staffed overflow scanning lane. With 5 gate teachers this costs THB 7,000.
2. The food court hits its lunch peakBring in an approved reserve vendor. The price depends on how short you are of food.
3. A storm arrives before the evening sessionUse covered backup and cap entry to fit. If you booked the rain plan, this costs THB 12,000 and loses no evening admissions.
4. A stage or headline act is running lateUse a rehearsed student set while crew prepares. With 3 on break cover it costs THB 6,500.
  1. After each click, check Working cash, Day, Evening, Guest experience and Refunds. If a choice is greyed out, you cannot afford it. Choose an affordable response. Call a safe stop is for when none can be paid for.

Make the last food sales decision

  1. After incident 4, read Live trading desk. Compare response cost, forecast meals and school commission. If queues limit sales, consider Open an express lane. If you want more buyers, compare Run a food-and-drink combo.
  2. In the worked example, choose Keep the current setup. Click Lock trading decision on its card. This takes you to 9 Results.

Game step 9 • Results

Results and common problems

Check your score before resetting

  1. Read Festival mission achieved or Festival mission incomplete. Check actual admissions and how much is available for charity. Write down guest experience out of 100.
  2. Read the Finance report. Compare ticket revenue after refunds, stall rent, food commission, sponsor income and costs. A Net surplus / deficit below zero means the festival lost money. The loan helps with cash while planning. Its fee counts as a cost.
  3. If you signed sponsors, check Sponsor audit. Look at Total earned, Bonus included and Deposit returned. Read the Incident log to explain any lost admissions.
  4. Show or screenshot the results for your teacher before clicking Run another festival or Reset game.
The worked example was tested across all three rival events. It finished with 3,900 admissions, 98/100 guest experience and more than THB 580,000 available for charity. Exact amounts can vary with bids and choices.

实际入场人数至少 3,001 人，至少有 100,000 泰铢可用于慈善，并且校园节安全结束。活动时间为 15:00 至 21:00。

做宣传并查看竞争活动

  1. 在三张卡片上点击 Run campaign：School newsletter & parent groups、Student reels & artist teasers、House and class presale。检查三个按钮都有勾。总费用为 57,000 泰铢，也符合通信赞助商的线上宣传要求。
  2. 在 Presale pulse 下点击 Run presale check（检查预售情况）。看是哪一场活动在竞争，以及哪个观众群体受影响最大。
  3. 按本例，找到 Keep the plan and protect your cash（维持计划、保留现金），点击该卡片的 Choose response。这项费用为 0 泰铢。若选择付费方案，观察预测有什么变化。
  4. 看页面下方的 tickets forecast（预计售票数）、票务收入以及日场和晚场的需求。本例预计售出 3,900 张。如果你用的是其他方案且预计销量低，可以只改一项票价或宣传，再比较结果。
  5. 点击 Open ticket sales（开始售票）。如果是灰色，确认已经检查预售并选了应对方案。接下来进入 7 Staff & safety。

  2. 按本例填写下表。如果你改了其他选择，各岗位人数至少要达到你自己屏幕上显示的最低要求。

预订服务并开场

  1. 在以下六张卡片上点击 Book service，确认每张都有勾：First-aid team、Professional security、Touring-grade stage, power & sound、Radios and public announcements、Free water and hygiene checks、Accessible routes and signage。
  2. 本例还要预订 Covered backup and rain plan（有遮盖的备用场地和雨天方案），费用为 78,000 泰铢。它是可选项，但能帮助应对暴雨。七项服务共 437,000 泰铢。
  3. 先看页面底部的提示。如果有警告，按提示补足教师、预订缺少的服务，或处理现金不足。保留一部分 Working cash，用于应对突发状况。
  4. 看到 Ready to open gates 后，点击 Open the festival（开场）。点击前检查计划，因为开场后不能再改前面的筹办步骤。

游戏第 8 步 • Event day

处理四个突发状况

每个状况选一个应对方案

  1. 看 Incident 1 of 4（第 1 个状况，共 4 个）卡片和上方数据。比较方案的费用，以及 tickets at risk（可能失去的入场人数）。入场人数下降可能产生退款，也可能影响赞助收入。
  2. 全组同意后，在选中的卡片上点击 Choose response。游戏会执行并进入下一个状况。看 Last incident，了解上一次选择造成的结果。
状况示例选择的卡片
1. 许多观众同时到达 5 号门Open a staffed overflow scanning lane。增开一条有人值守的验票通道。若入口安排了 5 位教师，费用为 7,000 泰铢。
2. 美食区迎来用餐高峰Bring in an approved reserve vendor。请符合要求的备用摊主增加供应，费用取决于缺少多少份。
3. 晚场开始前下暴雨Use covered backup and cap entry to fit。使用有遮盖的备用场地。若已预订雨天方案，此时支付 12,000 泰铢，晚场入场人数不减少。
4. 主秀或晚场节目延误Use a rehearsed student set while crew prepares。先安排排练好的学生节目。若有 3 人负责替岗，费用为 6,500 泰铢。
  1. 每次选择后，查看 Working cash（可用现金）、Day（日场）、Evening（晚场）、Guest experience（观众体验）和 Refunds（退款）。灰色选项表示付不起。若所有方案都付不起，选择 Call a safe stop（安全停办）。

决定最后的餐饮销售策略

  1. 第 4 个状况结束后，会看到 Live trading desk。比较各方案的费用、预计售出份数和学校分成。如果排队影响销量，可以考虑 Open an express lane（开快速通道）。想吸引更多买家，可比较 Run a food-and-drink combo（餐饮套餐）。
  2. 按本例选 Keep the current setup（维持原有安排）。在对应卡片上点击 Lock trading decision，进入 9 Results。

游戏第 9 步 • Results

查看结果并解决常见问题

重置前先记录成绩

  1. 看页面显示 Festival mission achieved（完成任务）还是 Festival mission incomplete（未完成任务）。记录实际入场人数、可用于慈善的金额，以及满分 100 的观众体验分。
  2. 看 Finance report（财务报告）。比较扣除退款后的票务收入、摊位租金、餐饮分成、赞助和费用。Net surplus / deficit（净盈余或亏损）小于零表示校园节亏损。借款可增加筹办时的现金，借款手续费计入成本。
  3. 如果签过赞助合同，查看 Sponsor audit。确认 Total earned（实际获得）、Bonus included（其中的奖金）以及 Deposit returned（退回的预付款）。用 Incident log（突发状况记录）说明为什么有人没入场。
  4. 先把结果展示给老师或截图，再点击 Run another festival 或 Reset game 开始新的一局。
这个示例用三种竞争活动分别测试过。结果均为 3,900 人入场、98/100 观众体验分，并有 超过 580,000 泰铢可用于慈善。具体金额随报价和选择而变。

Teacher notes and sources

Allow roughly 5 min to start, 12 min for bids, 13 min for scheduling, 13 min for stalls and sponsorship, 11 min for ticketing, 13 min for staffing, 15 min for live decisions and 8 min for debrief. Ask teams to defend one safety decision and explain which expense or sponsor made the biggest difference.

Real artist names and releases are used as classroom examples. Bids, availability, sponsors, costs, capacities and audience figures are fictional. The attendance goal measures total entries across two sessions, while operating limits apply at any one time.
