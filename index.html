Run the festival

Play in teams. Decide who runs Finance, Programme and Operations. Discuss each phase, then lock it in. You begin with a maximum starting budget of ฿3.1 million. Borrow up to ฿1 million separately, with an 8% finance fee. Earn additional money from ticket sales, stalls and sponsors. For a win, admit 3,001 or more people, finish safely and raise at least ฿100,000 for charity after covering your costs.

Allow roughly 5 min to start, 12 min for bids, 13 min for scheduling, 13 min for stalls and sponsorship, 11 min for ticketing, 13 min for staffing, 15 min for live decisions and 8 min for debrief. Ask teams to defend one safety decision and explain which expense or sponsor made the biggest difference.

Real artist names and releases are used as classroom examples. Bids, availability, sponsors, costs, capacities and audience figures are fictional. The attendance goal measures total entries across two sessions, while operating limits apply at any one time.
